# Yaoyao creative journey

A direct project-first portfolio covering creative technology, photography and video, research, and enterprise AI workflows.

## Structure and presentation

- The page opens with “Explore the projects” and three category links. The top-left wordmark is “Yaoyao creative journey.”
- MagicMask includes a small medal marking the 2021 Apple Worldwide Developers Conference Student Scholarship.
- The timeline uses larger, sentence-case discipline and project names: iOS development, XR, accessibility/HCI, generative AI, MyIDNet and 2026 AfterLight.
- AfterLight links to its GitHub Pages frontend prototype and four final photo/video collections. Collections are labeled **Free pilots**. The frontend demo is explicitly identified as a prototype with simulated processing; the delivered collections are presented separately.
- Apple’s work scope is presented as four headings only: enterprise rollout and approvals; evolving AI system evaluation and model validation; video workflow prototyping and standardization; Sales AI & Data Academy / training operations.
- The original AI voice prompt image and project video remain available through collapsed supporting-material sections.
- The job description is retained with small “why I include this” text explaining scope and roadmap context, and the distinction between the complete role and a builder motivated by curiosity across technology and design.

No supplier-specific implementation details, detailed operational claims or employment-arrangement labels are included in the public copy. The supplied resumes are not part of the package.

## Controls

Timeline projects, research notes and Apple supporting materials start collapsed. The AI voice prompt button opens its corresponding topic directly. “Collapse all” closes Apple supporting material and removes embedded playback. The remaining film categories and prototype examples retain their original links.

## Open and edit

Open `dist/index.html`, or `Selected-Work-Portfolio.html` in the parent workspace. External videos, project demos and Drive collections need internet access.

- `dist/index.html`: portfolio layout, timeline and source links.
- `dist/styles.css`: original visual and media components.
- `dist/story.css`: project-first layout, enlarged timeline type and pilot/JD components.
- `dist/app.js`: case headings, media, filtering and disclosure interactions.
- `python3 scripts/package_portfolio.py`: regenerate the standalone HTML, source ZIP and GitHub Pages deployment ZIP.
- `GITHUB-PAGES.md`: publish the static deployment package from a GitHub repository root.
